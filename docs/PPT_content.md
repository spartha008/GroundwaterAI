# PPT CONTENT — GroundWaterAI

## Slide 1 — Title
GroundWaterAI
AI-powered seasonal groundwater prediction and early-warning system

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Groundwater depletion is difficult to anticipate locally.
- Irregular monsoon patterns change recharge.
- Irrigation and extraction can accelerate depletion.
- Many monitoring systems are descriptive rather than predictive.

## Slide 3 — Proposed Solution
Combine groundwater history, rainfall, climate variables and extraction indicators.
Use ML to predict future groundwater depth.
Convert the prediction into a risk level.
Provide an actionable recommendation.
